## 🧬 About me

I'm a PhD candidate at **Bilkent University** (Konu Lab), working at the interface of **probabilistic modeling** and **functional genomics**. My research asks how genotype propagates into expression — and how we can read that signal back to find causal variants.

- 🔬 **Core question:** how do allelic imbalance and loss of heterozygosity reveal the genetic basis of mutant phenotypes?
- 📐 **Methods I build with:** beta-binomial hidden Markov models, Bayesian inference, differential-expression statistics
- 🐟 **Model system:** zebrafish (*Danio rerio*) — ENU mutagenesis, bulk segregant analysis (BSA), LOH mapping
- 🌍 **Beyond one species:** cross-species transcriptomics and conservation pipelines
- 💬 Ask me about **RNA-seq pipelines, ASE methodology, or NGS data engineering**
